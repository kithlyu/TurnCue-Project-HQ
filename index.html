Beta supervisor guide

The local pilot and internal alpha happen before the Philippines pilot. Do not enroll real customers until the project owner confirms launch readiness.

Identify one willing participating business and a point of contact
Document its current walk-in queue and busiest hours
Coordinate staff orientation and practice sessions
Keep the business's normal queue method available as backup
Observe customer understanding, missed turns and staff interventions
Send weekly feedback and report serious problems immediately
